Rhyme Factory

Rhyme Factory is a private production tool used only by the YouTube channel Puddlehop Lane, which publishes original sing-along songs for toddlers and preschoolers.

The tool creates the channel's song videos, uploads them to that one channel, and keeps a production log in the operator's own Google Sheet. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
